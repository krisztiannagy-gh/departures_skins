### London Underground skin

A new skin that looks like the amber platform indicators on the Tube, drawn with the real TfL dot-matrix font.

- **Two departure rows and a clock.** Row 1 shows the next train. Row 2 shows train 2, then trains 3 to 6 in turn (only those due within 30 minutes).
- **Minutes:** `5 mins`, `1 min`, and nothing under a minute. When a train has gone, the rows float out and the list moves up.
- **Every row animates on its own:** old text floats out to the top, new text floats in from the bottom.
- **Long names** scroll or are cut. Choose under the skin's settings.
- **The clock** shows the box's local time.
- **No trains:** the line and direction you enter are shown, centred. Leave both empty for just the clock.
- **Hide trains to:** list the other direction's destinations (e.g. `Morden, Kennington`), because T-Skylt doesn't say which platform a London train uses.

Needs Departures 0.995. Pick *London Underground* as the skin on the settings page.
