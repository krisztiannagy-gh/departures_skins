### Clean start after adding or changing a station

- When you add or change a station, the app draws its start-up screen (logo and station name) over the board. The skin now wipes it and redraws everything, so the clock is complete and the first departure is no longer covered.
- **New station:** the old trains disappear at once, the line and direction show, and the new station's departures are fetched straight away.
- Other settings changes also fetch fresh data straight away.

The box updates the skin by itself.
