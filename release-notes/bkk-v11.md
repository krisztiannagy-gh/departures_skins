### i and í as on the real boards

- **i** now has a small roof at the top left and a platform at the foot (dot, empty row, then the letter), drawn after the real BKK boards. It is 3 px wide instead of 1.
- **í** gets the same roof and platform under its accent and gap. **Í** stays as in v10.
- Names with an i become 2 px wider per i, so a long name may be shortened or cut a little earlier.

The box updates the skin by itself.
